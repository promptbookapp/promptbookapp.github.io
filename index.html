How it works

Three simple steps from idea to a post on your channel.

1

Choose a niche

Browse categories like Cinematic, Product & Food, Nature & Wildlife or Funny Animation, or search by keyword.

2

Copy a prompt

Tap Copy on any prompt. Each one is already written in detail, so you don't start from a blank page.

3

Create and post

Paste it into the AI tool you like, make your video or image, then post it on your channel.

Prompt Book gives you the prompts. You create the video with the AI tool of your choice and publish it yourself.

Questions

Is Prompt Book free?

Yes, the app is free to use. It contains ads.

Does the app create the videos?

No. Prompt Book gives you well-written prompts. You paste them into the AI tool you already use to create the video or image.

Does the app create my YouTube, TikTok, Facebook or Instagram account?

No. You create and manage your own accounts on each platform. Prompt Book helps you decide on a niche and gives you content ideas to start with.

Which AI tools can I use the prompts with?

Any tool that accepts text prompts. You can copy a prompt and paste it where you like.

Are new prompts added?

Yes. New prompts and categories are added regularly, and new ones are marked with a red "New" badge in the app.

Can I make money with my channel?

Results depend on your content, consistency and each platform's rules. Prompt Book does not promise any income. It simply makes it faster to get started.
